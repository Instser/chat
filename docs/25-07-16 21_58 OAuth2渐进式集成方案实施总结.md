## 🔧 配置实施

### application.yml配置
```yaml
oauth2:
  security:
    state-token-expiry: 300
    max-login-attempts: 5
    rate-limit-window: 3600
    encryption-key: ${OAUTH2_ENCRYPTION_KEY}
  redirect-urls:
    success: ${OAUTH2_SUCCESS_URL}
    error: ${OAUTH2_ERROR_URL}
  providers:
    google:
      client-id: ${GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID}
      client-secret: ${GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET}
    github:
      client-id: ${GITHUB_CLIENT_ID}
      client-secret: ${GITHUB_CLIENT_SECRET}
```
